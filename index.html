Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Add whatever you'd like to your cart, then tap "Checkout on WhatsApp." Your order opens as a pre-filled message straight to Nontobeko, who'll confirm stock, payment, and delivery with you directly.

What payment methods are accepted?

Payment is arranged directly with Nontobeko over WhatsApp once your order is confirmed — usually EFT or cash on delivery/collection, depending on your location.

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes. We're based in Jozini, KZN, and deliver across South Africa. Add your location at checkout and Nontobeko will confirm the delivery fee and timeframe with you on WhatsApp.

What sizes do the fragrances come in?

Every scent comes in 50ml (R250) and 30ml (R150), for both His and Hers ranges — same pricing either way.

Can I exchange or return a product?

If there's an issue with your order, message Nontobeko on WhatsApp as soon as it arrives so it can be sorted out quickly.

How do I become a reseller?

Visit the Join Dream Team page for stock pricing, fees, and benefits, then book a one-on-one session with Nontobeko to get started.
