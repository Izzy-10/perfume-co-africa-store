Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

The Perfume Co. Africa (run by Nontobeko Sibiya, "we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through this website and how it's used.

What we collect

When you use the cart, contact form, or "Book a Session" button, we collect what you choose to share: your name, phone number, the products you're ordering, your delivery location, and any message you send. This information is sent directly to Nontobeko via WhatsApp — it is not stored on this website or on any server.

How we use it

  • To confirm your order, pricing, and stock availability
  • To arrange payment and calculate delivery fees based on your location
  • To respond to general enquiries or Dream Team reseller questions

Payments

This website does not process or store any payment information. All payment arrangements are made directly between you and Nontobeko Sibiya via WhatsApp.

Cart data

Items you add to your cart are stored only in your own browser (using local storage) so your selection is remembered if you return. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted anywhere unless you tap "Checkout on WhatsApp."

Third parties

We do not sell or share your information with third parties. Messages sent via WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
